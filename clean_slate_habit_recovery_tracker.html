The Urge Surfing Method

An urge is like an ocean wave. It reaches a peak (lasting around 10–15 minutes) and then subsides. You do not need to fight or surrender to it—just ride it without acting.

  • Locate the physical sensation in your body without panicking.
  • Take deep slow breaths directly into the urge.
  • Remind yourself: "This feeling is temporary and will pass shortly."
